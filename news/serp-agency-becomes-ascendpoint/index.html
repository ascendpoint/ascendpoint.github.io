SERP Agency Rebrands as AscendPoint Agency; Sonia Hounsell Joins as Co-CEO

AscendPoint Agency · October 1, 2024

October 1, 2024 — SERP Agency today announced that it has rebranded as AscendPoint Agency, a holding company designed to bring together the best marketing service providers in healthcare.

Sonia Hounsell joins the business as Co-CEO to bring that vision to life. Sonia brings brand-building and business-building experience from senior roles at leading consumer and healthcare companies and from founding FunkkOFF!, the oral care brand featured on ABC's Shark Tank, which she led to a successful exit.

How AscendPoint is structured

  • SERP Dental continues as the operating brand focused on dental marketing.
  • AscendPoint Agency is the platform: shared leadership, technology, data, and operations.
  • AscendPoint is raising a fund to acquire additional specialist healthcare marketing agencies.

“Healthcare providers deserve marketing partners who understand their specialty. AscendPoint lets great specialist agencies keep their identity while gaining the brand, technology, and capital to grow.”

About AscendPoint Agency

AscendPoint Agency is a healthcare growth platform that builds and operates specialty marketing brands for local healthcare providers. Its operating brands, SERP Dental and Medical Marketing Whiz, help dental, aesthetics, and women's health practices acquire, retain, and grow patient relationships, backed by a shared AI, data, and operations platform. Learn more at ascendpoint.agency.
