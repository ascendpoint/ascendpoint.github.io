AI-Powered Business Solutions for the Growth-Focused Modern Dental Practice in 2026

By Sonia Hounsell · Mar 2, 2026

Insights from the DentWorker Circle

Based on a recent collaborative session within the DentWorker Circle, the pace of innovation on the business side of dentistry has never been faster. The DentWorker Circle represents a group of forward-thinking professionals in the dental industry, and by leveraging the combined intellectual capital of its members, the Circle recently explored the AI-driven tools that are transforming how practices handle scheduling, intake, phone management, referrals, and performance analytics. For the growth-focused practice owner, staying ahead isn't just about clinical skill. It's about knowing which AI partners will streamline your operations, reduce overhead, and free your team to focus on patient care. I've compiled the highlights from our discussion below.

Practice Performance Analytics

Pronto! by Maeva Advisors

Most practice owners know their numbers could be better but struggle to pinpoint exactly where the opportunities lie. Pronto! eliminates that guesswork. Its AI engine conducts a comprehensive evaluation of hundreds of clinical, financial, and operational metrics across your practice with a single click. Rather than wading through spreadsheets and dashboards, you receive a clear, prioritized action plan that identifies the most impactful opportunities for improving EBITDA and operational efficiency. This was one of the tools that generated the most discussion within the DentWorker Circle for its ability to surface insights that would otherwise require a dedicated analyst.

Best for: Practice owners and DSOs looking for a data-driven roadmap to profitability without hiring a full-time analyst.

Patient Intake and Communication

Elva AI: Conversational Digital Intake

Paper forms and clunky PDFs create friction for patients and busywork for staff. Elva AI replaces the traditional intake process with a conversational chat interface that guides patients through their medical history and insurance information in a natural, intuitive way. The platform syncs seamlessly with major Practice Management Systems, ensuring data flows directly into your existing workflow. Practices using Elva report a 90% reduction in administrative busywork and a significant improvement in form completeness, meaning fewer follow-up calls and fewer errors. Several members of the DentWorker Circle noted that the patient experience improvement alone made adoption worthwhile.

Best for: Practices seeking to modernize the patient experience from the very first touchpoint while freeing front-desk staff from data entry.

Dentina.ai: The 24/7 AI Receptionist

Every missed call is a potential missed appointment. Dentina.ai provides an AI-driven front office assistant capable of handling unlimited parallel calls around the clock. It answers inquiries, transcribes voicemails, and integrates directly with your CRM, updating patient records in real time. The result is that your human staff can devote their full attention to patients who are physically present in the office, while Dentina ensures no caller goes unanswered.

Best for: High-volume practices and multi-location groups that lose revenue to missed or after-hours calls.

Referral Management

Bright Referral and Share to Care

The traditional paper referral pad is an analog bottleneck in an increasingly digital workflow. Bright Referral uses smart cards that general practitioners can tap against a patient's phone to deliver an instant, custom referral experience. The patient receives the information they need to book immediately, while the specialist gains real-time analytics on referral network performance.

Best for: Specialists looking to grow and track their referral pipelines.

Getting Started

The shift to an AI-enhanced practice doesn't require an overnight overhaul. Start by identifying your biggest operational pain point, whether that's missed calls, slow intake, opaque financials, or leaky referral pipelines, and pilot one solution that directly addresses it. Measure the impact over a quarter, then expand. As we discussed in the DentWorker Circle, the practices that thrive in 2026 and beyond will be those that treat their back office with the same precision and innovation they bring to the chair.
