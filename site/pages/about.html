Our story

From one agency to a platform.

AscendPoint began in 2023, when Patrick Hounsell founded Fletcher Road Capital to acquire specialized marketing agencies. In January 2024 it acquired SERP Agency, and that April SERP Dental launched as a dental-specific agency focused on practice growth outcomes.

In October 2024, Sonia Hounsell joined as Co-CEO and SERP Agency became AscendPoint Agency, a holding company built to bring together the best marketing service providers in healthcare.

Since then, SERP Dental acquired SparkLyfe, AscendPoint launched the AscendVu AI product suite, and in September 2026 Medical Marketing Whiz joined as AscendPoint's second operating brand, keeping its name, its team, and its founder, Lori Werner.

Today AscendPoint builds and acquires specialist marketing brands and gives each one the AI, data, and operating muscle to help local providers acquire, retain, and grow patient relationships.

Medical Marketing Whiz

Lori Werner

Founder & Chief Marketing Whiz, Medical Marketing Whiz

Lori Werner is the Founder and Chief Marketing Whiz of Medical Marketing Whiz (MMW), a healthcare marketing agency specializing in women’s health, longevity medicine, functional medicine, and aesthetics. Since founding MMW in 2016, Lori has grown the company from a one-woman operation into a team of more than 20 marketing professionals serving over 500 practice locations nationwide. In September 2026, MMW was acquired by AscendPoint, where Lori continues to lead growth and bring MMW’s specialized expertise to a broader network of healthcare providers.

Before launching MMW, Lori spent more than 15 years in medical device and pharmaceutical sales with industry leaders including Hologic and Sanofi. That time in the field gave her a firsthand understanding of how physicians think, how practices operate, and what it truly takes to turn new technology and services into patient demand. It also shaped the philosophy that defines MMW today: authentic, results-driven marketing that puts patient education and credibility ahead of hype.

Under Lori’s leadership, MMW built what is widely regarded as the most comprehensive marketing program in the industry, combining strategy, branding, websites, SEO, paid media, social media, content, and patient education into a single integrated solution designed for specialty practices. Her close partnerships with leading medical device companies have helped hundreds of practices successfully launch and grow new service lines.

Lori is a three-time author, including Anti-Aging and Longevity Marketing, a Forbes contributor, and a sought-after speaker at major medical conferences across the country, where she shares practical strategies for practice growth and patient engagement. She is also a certified menopause coach, a credential that reflects her deep commitment to women’s health and to helping practices better serve patients through every stage of life.

Her mission is simple: empowering patients through personalized connections with leading specialists.
