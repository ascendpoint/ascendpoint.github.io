AscendPoint Acquires Medical Marketing Whiz

AscendPoint Agency · September 1, 2026

September 1, 2026 — AscendPoint Agency today announced that it has acquired Medical Marketing Whiz (MMW), the women-led marketing agency serving private medical practices in aesthetics and women's health.

Founded by Lori Werner, Chief Marketing Whiz, MMW has spent more than 20 years helping private-practice physicians attract their ideal patients and keep them for life. The agency serves OB/GYN, urogynecology, functional and naturopathic medicine, med spa and aesthetics, and weight loss practices. It was named Best Women's Health Marketing Agency by the International Society of Cosmetogynecology (ISCG) in 2022 and again in 2023.

What stays the same, and what gets better

  • MMW keeps its name, its team, and its founder. Lori Werner continues to lead the brand.
  • MMW becomes AscendPoint's second operating brand, alongside SERP Dental.
  • MMW clients gain access to AscendPoint's shared platform: AI search intelligence, tracking and attribution, and operations.
  • SERP Dental gains MMW's retention capabilities: email and SMS nurture, reputation management, webinars and events, podcasting, and book publishing.

About AscendPoint Agency

AscendPoint Agency is a healthcare growth platform that builds and operates specialty marketing brands for local healthcare providers. Its operating brands, SERP Dental and Medical Marketing Whiz, help dental, aesthetics, and women's health practices acquire, retain, and grow patient relationships, backed by a shared AI, data, and operations platform.
