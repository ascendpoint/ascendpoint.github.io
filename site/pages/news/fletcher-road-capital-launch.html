Patrick Hounsell Launches Fletcher Road Capital to Acquire Specialized Marketing Agencies

AscendPoint Agency · February 24, 2023

February 24, 2023 — Agency executive Patrick Hounsell today launched Fletcher Road Capital, an investment company formed to acquire small and mid-sized marketing agencies with under $2 million in annual revenue.

Fletcher Road Capital is focused on a specific kind of agency: firms that specialize in one industry. Specialist agencies tend to know their clients' economics, regulations, and customers better than generalists do, but they often lack the capital, systems, and leadership depth to scale. Fletcher Road Capital was created to close that gap by acquiring strong specialist agencies and giving them the operating muscle to grow.

The investment thesis

  • Acquire founder-led agencies under $2 million in revenue.
  • Prioritize agencies that specialize in a single industry.
  • Keep what makes each agency great, and add capital, technology, and operating discipline.

“The best agencies I know are specialists. They understand their clients' businesses deeply, but they rarely have the capital or infrastructure to scale. Fletcher Road Capital exists to give great specialist agencies that next level.”

— Patrick Hounsell, Founder, Fletcher Road CapitalDraft quote · pending approval

Fletcher Road Capital would go on to acquire SERP Agency in January 2024, the first step toward what is now AscendPoint Agency.

About AscendPoint Agency

AscendPoint Agency is a healthcare growth platform that builds and operates specialty marketing brands for local healthcare providers. Its operating brands, SERP Dental and Medical Marketing Whiz, help dental, aesthetics, and women's health practices acquire, retain, and grow patient relationships, backed by a shared AI, data, and operations platform.
