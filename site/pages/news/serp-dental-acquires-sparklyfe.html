SERP Dental™ Acquires SparkLyfe™

AscendPoint Agency · August 2025

August 2025 — SERP Dental™, the dental operating brand of AscendPoint™ Agency, today announced that it has acquired SparkLyfe™, a leading provider in the dental marketing space focused on driving Google Maps rankings and outcomes.

For most dental practices, the Google Maps results are where new patients decide who to call. SparkLyfe built its reputation on winning those rankings for dental practices. Bringing SparkLyfe into SERP Dental deepens SERP Dental's local search capability and gives more practices access to a proven maps playbook.

What the acquisition means for practices

  • A stronger Google Maps and Google Business Profile program inside SERP Dental.
  • SparkLyfe clients gain access to SERP Dental's full suite: SEO, Google Ads, websites, and lead tracking.
  • One team measured on the outcome that matters most: new patients.

“Google Maps is where most new-patient decisions start. SparkLyfe is great at winning there, and together we can deliver that result to many more practices.”

— Patrick Hounsell, Co-CEO, AscendPoint Agency

About AscendPoint Agency

AscendPoint™ Agency is a healthcare marketing company that acquires and grows specialized healthcare marketing agencies. Its operating brands, SERP Dental™ and Medical Marketing Whiz®, help dental, aesthetics, and women's health practices acquire, retain, and grow patient relationships, backed by a shared AI, data, and operations platform. Together, they share one purpose: to be a platform to help businesses grow by delivering results that matter and creating lasting value for clients and team members alike. Learn more at ascendpoint.agency.
