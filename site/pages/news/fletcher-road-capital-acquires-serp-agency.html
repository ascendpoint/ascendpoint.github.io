Fletcher Road Capital Acquires SERP Agency

AscendPoint Agency · January 4, 2024

January 4, 2024 — Fletcher Road Capital today announced its acquisition of SERP.AGENCY, a search marketing agency serving dental practices, home services companies, and B2B businesses.

SERP Agency helps clients win new customers and patients through search engine optimization and website optimization, turning search visibility into booked appointments and qualified leads. The acquisition is Fletcher Road Capital's first, and it establishes the operating base for the company's platform strategy.

Why SERP Agency

  • A proven search and website optimization practice with measurable client outcomes.
  • A strong and growing base of dental clients, a vertical with clear economics and high lifetime patient value.
  • A team and delivery model that can scale with added capital and leadership.

“SERP Agency has what we look for: a team that delivers real results and a client base that trusts them. Our job is to give them the resources to do more of what they already do well.”

— Patrick HounsellDraft quote · pending approval

Within three months of the acquisition, SERP Agency would sharpen its focus on dentistry with the launch of SERP Dental.

About AscendPoint Agency

AscendPoint Agency is a healthcare growth platform that builds and operates specialty marketing brands for local healthcare providers. Its operating brands, SERP Dental and Medical Marketing Whiz, help dental, aesthetics, and women's health practices acquire, retain, and grow patient relationships, backed by a shared AI, data, and operations platform. Learn more at ascendpoint.agency.
