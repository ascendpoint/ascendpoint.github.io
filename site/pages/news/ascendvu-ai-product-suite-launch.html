AscendPoint Launches the AscendVu AI Product Suite

AscendPoint Agency · March 1, 2026

March 1, 2026 — AscendPoint Agency today launched the AscendVu AI product suite. The suite includes a dental-first AI visibility tool that helps practices understand how they measure up in AI search, then recommends the content to create to increase their visibility and rankings.

Patients increasingly ask AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google's AI Overviews to recommend a dentist. Most practices have no idea whether they are being recommended, or why a competitor is named instead. AscendVu answers both questions.

What AscendVu does

  • Measures AI visibility: where and how often a practice appears in AI answers for the searches that matter in its market.
  • Benchmarks competitors: which nearby practices AI tools recommend instead, and why.
  • Recommends content: specific pages, answers, and updates that raise visibility and rankings.
  • Tracks progress: AI visibility over time, alongside traditional SEO.
