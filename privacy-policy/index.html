Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

This policy explains how AscendPoint Agency (“AscendPoint,” “we,” “us”) handles information collected through ascendpoint.agency (the “Site”). Our operating brands, SERP Dental and Medical Marketing Whiz, run their own websites with their own privacy policies.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you fill out a form, apply for a strategy session, or book a call, we collect what you enter, such as your name, email address, phone number, practice or company name, and your message or answers.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, the Site uses cookies and similar technologies to understand how it is used. This can include your IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, the page that referred you, and campaign parameters in the link you followed (for example, utm_source).

How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiry, schedule and hold calls, and connect you with the right AscendPoint brand.
  • To send information you asked for, and, where permitted, follow-up communication about our services. You can opt out at any time.
  • To measure and improve the Site and our marketing, including which campaigns bring visitors to us.
  • To protect the Site, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations.

Service providers we use

We use trusted third parties to run parts of the Site. They process information on our behalf and under their own privacy terms:

  • Typeform for contact and application forms.
  • OnceHub for booking calls.
  • Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics for measuring Site usage.
  • Advertising platforms such as Google and Meta, which may use cookies to measure and improve our ads.
  • Our hosting provider, which serves the Site and keeps standard server logs.

We may share information with our operating brands (SERP Dental and Medical Marketing Whiz) so the right team can help you. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and the Site will still work. You can opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, and manage ad personalization in your Google and Meta ad settings.

Keeping information safe

The Site is served only over encrypted HTTPS connections. We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as the law requires.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Children

The Site is intended for business owners and professionals. It is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of the page shows when it last changed.
