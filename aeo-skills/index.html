Install

Five minutes, once.

In Claude

Go to Customize > Skills in claude.ai or the Claude app, upload each ZIP from the For-Claude folder, and turn the skills on. Then just ask: “Is my website blocking ChatGPT?”

In ChatGPT

On Business or Enterprise, upload the same ZIPs under Plugins > Skills. On any plan with Projects, paste the included instructions and add the eight playbook files. Step-by-step guide included.

Three rules the skills follow: no patient information goes into any AI tool, a person approves everything before it is published, and anything unverified is marked [CONFIRM]. General marketing guidance, not legal or compliance advice.
